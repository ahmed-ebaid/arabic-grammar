# إعراب Curriculum Plan

## Learning goal

Teach a learner to infer the correct reading of unvocalized Arabic by combining sentence structure, word form, agreement, high-frequency vocabulary, and context. Each lesson is short and interactive; each concept returns later with less tashkeel and more realistic text.

## Progression rules

- Lessons unlock at 70% mastery; the best score is retained.
- Wrong answers have no penalty and always include an explanation.
- New concepts begin fully vocalized, move to final-vowel-only support, and then appear unvocalized.
- Every unit mixes recognition, role identification, ending selection, and reading.
- Vocabulary is limited and recycled so grammar—not unknown words—is the main difficulty.
- Essential صرف is introduced where it improves reading; it is not taught as an isolated theory course.

## Level 1 — Reading foundations

**Outcome:** Notice that endings carry meaning and recognize the basic shape of simple sentences.

1. Why endings change: role, state, and visible ending.
2. اسم، فعل، حرف: identify the three word kinds.
3. المبتدأ والخبر: both normally مرفوع.
4. الفعل والفاعل: find who performed an action.
5. المفعول به: distinguish the receiver of an action.
6. حروف الجر: predict الجر after common prepositions.
7. First reading checkpoint: short nominal and verbal sentences without tashkeel.

## Level 2 — Nouns and agreement

**Outcome:** Read common noun phrases and nominal sentences by recognizing agreement and noun markers.

1. Definite and indefinite nouns.
2. Gender: masculine and feminine markers.
3. Number: singular, dual, and sound plurals.
4. Adjective agreement in definiteness, gender, number, and case.
5. الإضافة: مضاف and مضاف إليه.
6. Demonstratives and nearby nouns.
7. Pronouns and attached possessive pronouns.
8. Reading checkpoint: descriptions, signs, and short factual paragraphs.

## Level 3 — The verbal system

**Outcome:** Use verb form, tense, and subject agreement to parse common verbal sentences.

1. Past and present verbs.
2. Subject pronouns hidden inside verb forms.
3. Common present-tense prefixes and suffixes.
4. Direct objects and two-object patterns.
5. Negation with لا، ما، لم، لن.
6. Present-tense نصب and جزم.
7. Commands and prohibitions.
8. Essential derived-form recognition for high-frequency verbs.
9. Reading checkpoint: short narratives with progressively removed tashkeel.

## Level 4 — Expanded sentences

**Outcome:** Track grammatical roles when sentences contain modifiers, connectors, and governing particles.

1. كان وأخواتها.
2. إن وأخواتها.
3. Relative pronouns and relative clauses.
4. Coordination with و، ف، ثم، أو، لكن.
5. Adverbs of time and place.
6. الحال and simple descriptive circumstances.
7. Exceptions with إلا.
8. Numbers and counted nouns.
9. Reading checkpoint: connected prose and dialogue.

## Level 5 — Modern Arabic reading

**Outcome:** Read accessible contemporary prose without tashkeel and justify likely endings.

1. News and informational sentence patterns.
2. Headlines and omitted elements.
3. Passive voice in common modern usage.
4. Verbal nouns and participles.
5. Longer noun phrases and nested إضافة.
6. Punctuation and discourse clues.
7. Ambiguity: when context permits more than one reading.
8. Guided articles with tap-to-explain إعراب.
9. Mastery project: independently read and annotate a short modern passage.

## Level 6 — Classical Arabic reading

**Outcome:** Approach selected classical prose with appropriate caution and deeper analysis.

1. Classical word order and flexible placement.
2. Omitted subjects and understood predicates.
3. Conditional structures.
4. Emphasis, restriction, and fronting.
5. Weak verbs and irregular morphology needed for recognition.
6. Common classical particles and constructions.
7. وقف and وصل: how pausing affects audible endings.
8. Guided original classical-style passages with grammatical commentary.
9. Mastery project: read a short passage, propose its endings, and explain the evidence.

## Level 7 — Introduction to Alfiyyat Ibn Malik

**Outcome:** Connect advanced grammatical analysis to selected verses from the
public-domain Alfiyya while distinguishing the original poem from this app's
modern teaching explanations.

1. How to study a concise grammatical poem.
2. Speech, words, and the signs of nouns, verbs, and particles.
3. Mu'rab and mabni words.
4. Primary and secondary signs of i'rab.
5. Duals, sound plurals, and the five nouns.
6. Nominal governors in selected verses.
7. Verbal governors in selected verses.
8. Reading checkpoint: explain a selected verse and transfer its rule to new prose.

## Assessment model

- **Lesson mastery:** first-attempt accuracy across varied questions.
- **Unit checkpoint:** new sentences, not memorized lesson examples.
- **Reading fluency:** accuracy improves as tashkeel support decreases.
- **Retention:** previous concepts reappear in later lessons.
- **Explanation:** advanced checkpoints ask why a reading is likely, not only which vowel to choose.

## Content safety and quality

- All teaching copy and exercises are original or based on public-domain grammatical rules.
- Beta lessons may be tested before formal review but remain marked `pendingReview`.
- Production promotion requires review by a qualified Arabic grammar teacher.
- Beta Level 6 uses original classical-style examples rather than Qur'anic quotations.
- Level 7 attributes every quoted Alfiyya verse to Ibn Malik and uses only
  original explanations, translations, feedback, and exercises.
- Modern copyrighted commentaries are not copied.
- Future sourced classical or Qur'anic examples require exact citation and must not imply a single analysis where recognized analyses differ.

## Deferred scope

- AI-generated free-text إعراب.
- Accounts, cloud sync, subscriptions, and monetization.
- Teacher-review mode.
- Speech scoring and pronunciation recognition.
