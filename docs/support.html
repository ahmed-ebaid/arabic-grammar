Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the App for?

إعراب is for beginners who recognize Arabic letters and want to understand why word endings use fatha, damma, kasra, sukun, or substitute grammatical signs.

How do I change the language or theme?

Open Settings from the App and select Arabic or English and your preferred light, dark, or system theme.

Can I use the App offline?

Yes. Released lessons and quizzes are bundled with the App, and learning progress is stored on your device.

Does the App analyze my own Arabic text?

Not in the current beta. AI-powered sentence analysis is planned only after the teacher-reviewed learning curriculum is validated.

How do I remove my local data?

Use the reset-progress control when available. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored settings and learning progress, subject to your device backup settings.
