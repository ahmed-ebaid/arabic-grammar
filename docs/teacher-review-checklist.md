# Arabic Grammar Teacher Review Checklist

Use this checklist for every lesson version. The reviewer should be qualified
to teach Arabic grammar and should review both Arabic and English content.

## Grammar

- [ ] Every grammatical role is correct in its sentence context.
- [ ] Every حالة الإعراب or verb mood is correct.
- [ ] Every علامة الإعراب and displayed ending is correct.
- [ ] Vocalized and unvocalized forms correspond.
- [ ] Token boundaries cover the intended Arabic words.
- [ ] The lesson does not imply a unique parse where the text is ambiguous.

## Teaching Quality

- [ ] Terminology is introduced before it is used.
- [ ] Arabic and English explanations communicate the same rule.
- [ ] The difficulty is appropriate for beginners who know Arabic letters.
- [ ] Every incorrect option explains the learner's likely mistake.
- [ ] Objectives are measurable and covered by the exercises.

## Sources and Rights

- [ ] The source list reflects every work consulted.
- [ ] Public-domain claims refer to the underlying classical work, not an
      unlicensed modern edition or translation.
- [ ] No modern textbook wording, translation, exercise, or distinctive lesson
      arrangement was copied without permission.
- [ ] Original content is identified as original.

## Approval

- [ ] Requested corrections were applied.
- [ ] `contentVersion` identifies the exact reviewed version.
- [ ] Reviewer name and review timestamp are present.
- [ ] Review status may be changed to `approved`.
